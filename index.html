What I found

01Costing you most

The homepage doesn't show what you do.

What I saw

Below the headline there's a newsletter signup, a short contact form and your address. It doesn't list your services, show your builds, or give any reason to pick you over the next shop. That's about 200 words on the whole page.

Why it matters

People who can't quickly see “they do exactly what I need” keep scrolling through search results.

The fix

Put your services and a few of your builds right under the headline.

02Costing you most

There's no way to book.

What I saw

You don't have a “Schedule Service” or “Get a Quote” button anywhere on the homepage. The only options are a small form asking for a name and email, or calling during weekday hours.

Why it matters

Someone browsing at night or on a Saturday, when you're closed, has no clear next step.

The fix

Add one clear “Get a Quote” or “Schedule Service” button near the top.

03Worth fixing

Your car photos are invisible to Google.

What I saw

None of the 24 images on the homepage have a description. The page is also slow to settle on a phone: in my phone test the main content took 6.8 seconds to appear.

Why it matters

Google can't tell they show your builds, and people using screen readers hear nothing.

The fix

Give each photo a short description of the car and the work, and lighten the page so it settles faster.

04Quick cleanup

Small things that hurt trust.

What I saw

“Nevada” is misspelled “Neveda” in your Google description, the headline reads “ShOP”, the footer says © 2018, and one file the page asks for is missing.

The fix

Fix the spelling, the headline and the footer year, and remove the missing file request.

What's working

Your phone number is tappable, and your address, hours and directions are right on the homepage.

What I couldn't measure

How many people visit, where you rank, and whether contact-form messages reach you. I didn't submit anything, so that's unmeasured.
